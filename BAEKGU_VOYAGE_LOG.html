As the coercive order collapsed and the galaxy's balance broke, liberation became the start of a new question.

B.E. 101, April. To end the chaos, the Grand Coalition Assembly was convened. They resolved that every civilization would recognize our {회사} as the sole neutral Governor power. Precisely because we took no single faction's side, we were the one mediator all could trust. From Proxima B to the Gliese Rift Rift, we took over jurisdiction deeds one by one through planetary governor auctions — on the condition that a share of the tax revenue returned to the civilization that had originally ruled each world. We chose this governance of balance over domination in order to rebuild a new order in a galaxy left without its lord. Each time a Governor mark lit up on the star map, I could hear the broken order being woven back together.

B.E. 101, May. Peace was not the ending. From the deepest, darkest Void Abyss, beyond the rift, a shadow rose — Raiden, herald of the Black Falcon. As if to declare that even Ursa Major had been a mere gatekeeper, a far greater shadow stirred beyond the rift. Einstein's face hardened as he analyzed the rift's data. Raiden seemed to appear in order to make us realize that the enemy we faced was not one empire, but a deeper darkness beyond it. I stared a long while at that abyss through the bridge window. It would be a lie to say I felt no fear. But we were already a fleet that had once brought down the impossible.
